# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Multi-persona SaaS foundation — actual users depend on which tenant and modules are deployed. Typical personas include operations managers running tasks and approvals, HR / people leads using AI-generated employee analytics, and developers building or extending their own modules on top of the platform. What they share: they are in a task, not browsing. They expect the interface to keep up.

## Product Purpose

Seta Agentic Platform is an open-source, AI-first, multi-tenant enterprise platform foundation. Every business module embeds an autonomous agent that reads current system state, reasons across domains, and proposes concrete transactional actions. The human authorizes; the agent executes. Success looks like a platform where any tenant can stand up a capable, trustworthy AI-augmented work surface without rebuilding the plumbing.

## Brand Personality

Bold · Capable · Trustworthy. The platform earns trust by doing real work precisely — not by looking impressive. The interface should feel like a tool you'd stake a business on: confident in its hierarchy, zero-noise in its chrome, and decisive in its affordances. No hedging, no decoration for decoration's sake.

## Anti-references

- **Bloated enterprise SaaS** (SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow) — crowded, overly structured, legacy-feeling dashboards. Every panel should justify its existence.
- **AI product hype UI** — floating gradient orbs, neon glow accents, chat-only-focused design. The AI is the engine, not the aesthetic.
- **Heavy BI dashboards** (Power BI, Tableau) — chart-first, data-overloaded, not action-oriented. Surface data to enable action, not to impress.

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the work.** Earned familiarity over novelty. The interface vocabulary should feel immediately legible to anyone fluent in Linear, Raycast, or Vercel — not because we copied them, but because we solved the same problems.
2. **Confidence without decoration.** Bold and trustworthy means substance. No atmospheric gradients, no glow accents, no decorative motion. Visual weight signals importance, not style.
3. **Human-AI handoff is always legible.** Every agent action, approval gate, and HITL moment must be visually unambiguous. The user must always know what the agent proposes versus what is already committed.
4. **Modularity is first-class.** The platform adapts to its tenant. Design patterns must scale gracefully whether a module is a simple task list or a complex staffing workflow — no single module owns the visual language.
5. **Density serves the task.** Information density is a feature, not a problem to solve. Show what the user needs at the moment they need it — don't hide data behind progressive disclosure that adds clicks without adding clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort: reasonable contrast ratios (targeting WCAG 2.1 AA for all text), full keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility for primary flows. No formal compliance certification required. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
